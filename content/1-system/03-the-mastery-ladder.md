Your tracker has a mastery percentage. These definitions make it mean something. Grade after every session. Never grade upward on a feeling.

| Level | Definition | % | Worth |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L0 — Untouched | Not attempted. | 0 | Zero |
| L1 — Assisted | Solved with editorial, hint, or a peek. You understood a solution; you did not produce one. | 20 | Zero |
| L2 — Unaided once | Solved alone at least once, slowly, possibly after several wrong attempts. | 40 | Low |
| L3 — Cold re-solve | Re-solved correctly ≥3 days later. No notes, no hints, first attempt essentially working. | 60 | Real |
| L4 — Timed & explained | Cold re-solve inside the bar (E 10 / M 20 / H 35 min). Can state complexity and one alternative. | 80 | OA-ready |
| L5 — Teachable | Derives the template on a blank page, explains why it works, names two variants and the edge case that breaks the naive version. | 100 | Interview-ready |

### The five grading rules

- **Any hint drops a level.** Peeked at your own note? L2, not L3. Your notes are a hint.
- **Levels expire.** An L3 untouched for 30 days is an L2. Put a date next to every grade or the grade is fiction.
- **Report L3+, not solved.** When you tell yourself where you stand, use the L3+ count. Today: 111 vs roughly 9.
- **You do not need L5 everywhere.** L5 on the 19 templates and the 39 anchors. L3 on the rest. That distribution is what a strong candidate actually looks like.
- **Grade down freely.** Downgrading costs nothing and buys accuracy. Upgrading on a feeling costs an offer.

## 3.1 The 60-second audit — measure your real state in 20 minutes

- Pick 20 problems you solved 3+ weeks ago, across at least 8 patterns.
- For each: 60-second timer. Do not solve. Say out loud — pattern, data structure, loop shape, complexity.
- Score: instant / got there / wrong / no idea.
- Anything below "instant" is L1 or L2 regardless of what the tracker says.
- Do this before you touch §7. Twenty minutes buys you a targeted plan instead of a generic one.
