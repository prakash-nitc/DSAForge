## The first three minutes of the OA

- Read ALL problems before writing a line.
- Rank by (confidence × points) ÷ time. Start with the best ratio, not the first on screen.
- Can't name a pattern in three minutes? Flag and move on.
- Bank a full solve before attempting a partial.
- Never leave a problem blank. A brute force with correct I/O scores; an empty editor does not.
