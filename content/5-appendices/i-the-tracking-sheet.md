## Weekly review — five questions, thirty minutes, every Sunday

- L3+ count this week vs last week. Is the real number moving?
- Interleaved test score out of 10, and how many were "didn't identify".
- Which pattern produced the most failures? That one leads next week.
- Project: one sentence on what actually shipped.
- Energy and sleep trend. Any three-day pattern that needs addressing.
