You solve in Java and you will interview in Java. Syntax hesitation costs real minutes in a timed OA and reads as inexperience in a live round. This part exists so that none of it is ever the bottleneck.

> [!TIP]
> **Where to start**
>
> If the syntax itself is what stalls you, work forward from §23: signatures and return types, conversions, then one page per structure — arrays, lists, strings, StringBuilder, stack and queue, maps and sets — and §31 for loops, operators and error messages.
>
> §18 to §22 sit on top of that: what to use, the idioms, the traps, and what a round is actually judging. Nothing in this part is covered by Cover templates, because looking syntax up is the point.
